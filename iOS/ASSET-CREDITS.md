# Reset scene artwork

The app's Codex and Clawd characters are the existing bundled pet sprites. The scene props in `App/Assets.xcassets` are transparent PNGs created with the built-in image generator, using those pet sprites as style references. The prompts asked for crisp pixel art with no character, text, or background:

- `ResetPlane`: a blue and gold stunt biplane with an empty cockpit, viewed from the side.
- `ResetTokenPool`: a wide turquoise pool bordered by stacked gold quota tokens.
- `ResetSplash`: a turquoise cannonball splash that throws tokens into the air.
- `ResetParachute`: a blue and coral parachute carrying a star-marked treasure crate.
- `ResetBubble`: an iridescent pixel-art soap-bubble rim with a transparent center.

`ResetConfetti` is a single frame extracted from jellyfizh's [Confetti Effect Spritesheet](https://opengameart.org/content/confetti-effect-spritesheet), released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The original sheet is not included.
