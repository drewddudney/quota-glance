# Provider artwork

- `CodexPet.webp` is the original `codex-spritesheet-v6` asset bundled with the installed Codex desktop. The native view selects its idle, blink, and original four-frame wave cells.
- `ClawdIdle.png`, `ClawdWink.png`, and `ClawdWave.png` are unmodified decoded frames 0, 6, and 3 from Claude desktop's bundled `ion-dist/images/install-hub/clawd-laptop.webm`. The app selects the source viewport `(736, 1050, 1200, 800)` and scales it to fit. The silhouette, eyes, proportions, colour, and wink are from the original animation. There is no traced or procedurally drawn substitute.
- The source applications and authentication data are not modified.
